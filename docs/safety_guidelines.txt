
Traffic Safety Guidelines

1. Identify visible road users and vehicles before assessing a traffic scene.
2. A helmet may be visible on a motorcyclist, but image detection alone does not prove that the helmet meets safety standards.
3. A vehicle or person detection is not sufficient evidence of a traffic violation.
4. Seatbelt compliance should only be assessed when the seatbelt and relevant occupant are clearly visible.
5. If an image is unclear or evidence is insufficient, report uncertainty instead of making a definite claim.
6. Object detection confidence indicates the model's confidence in a detection, not the probability that a person is following traffic rules.
7. Bounding-box coordinates describe the detected object's location in the image.
